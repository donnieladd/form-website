privacy.

last updated 1 august 2026

This page describes what happens to information you send through formintel.co. It is written to be accurate about how the site actually works, not to cover every hypothetical.

what this site collects

The site does not run analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking scripts. It does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser between visits.

The only information form. receives is what you choose to type into the project inquiry form on the contact page — typically your name, work email, organization, role, and a description of what you need. Submitting that form opens an email addressed to form.; nothing is written to a database on this site.

how it's used

Inquiry information is used to respond to you and to scope potential work. That's the whole purpose. form. does not sell, rent, or share it with third parties for marketing, and does not add you to a mailing list from a project inquiry.

how it's stored

Inquiries live in form.'s business email. They're retained as long as they're commercially relevant — an active conversation, a live engagement, or the records of a completed one. You can ask for yours to be deleted at any time.

third parties

The site loads typefaces from Fontshare and Google Fonts. Those providers may receive your IP address and basic request information as part of serving the font files, under their own privacy terms. form. receives nothing from them.

The site is served through a hosting provider that, like all web hosts, processes standard server request logs.

your choices

  • Ask what form. holds about you
  • Ask for it to be corrected or deleted
  • Ask form. to stop contacting you

Email hello@formintel.co for any of the above. If you're covered by GDPR, CCPA, or a similar regime, those requests are honored as a matter of course.

children

This is a business site and isn't directed at children. form. does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it.
