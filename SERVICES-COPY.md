under pressure, and AI-native engineering. That combination is why the

## What form. doesn't do

Audit, tax, legal, and regulatory/forensic services — these require
licensure form. doesn't hold. Enterprise cloud infrastructure, DevOps,
NOC, FinOps, and large-scale data-platform consulting (Databricks,
Snowflake) — these require a dedicated infrastructure team form. doesn't
have yet. We say so upfront rather than claim it and figure it out on a
client's dime.
