what support covers

three territories.

Support is broader than a help desk and broader than an assistant. Most organizations need part of this list, not all of it — and we would rather right-size it than sell you a tier.

[ 01 ]

Human support

The capacity layer. Executive, virtual and administrative assistance, scheduling and calendar management, inbox and follow-up, documentation, client and project coordination, research, and the human-in-the-loop steps a workflow still needs a person for.

“There is too much work depending on too few people.”

[ 02 ]

Systems support

The technical stewardship layer. Website and application support, platform, user and SaaS administration, troubleshooting, automation and workflow maintenance, AI system support and operations, integrations, monitoring, permissions, vendor coordination and documentation.

“We built it. Who is going to keep it working?”

[ 03 ]

Operational support

The recurring execution layer between the two. Workflow and CRM administration, database upkeep, project coordination, documentation systems, reporting administration, follow-up systems, implementation assistance, task routing and quality-control workflows.

“The system exists, but somebody still has to operate it.”

how it works

what you agree to up front.

Support is only worth buying if the terms are clear before anything breaks. These get written down at the start, not negotiated during an outage — and the work is operated, not merely monitored. Changes get made, not just reported to you.

  • What is covered, and explicitly what is not
  • Response expectations by severity
  • Who to contact, and the escalation path
  • A named person who knows your environment
  • An inventory of systems, access and owners
  • A monthly record of what was actually done
  • Quarterly review against what you actually asked for
where support stops

it is not a dumping ground.

Every recurring task in a company could be labelled “support”. That would make the word useless and the accountability worse. Ownership stays where the expertise is.

Creative campaign execution belongs to form. creative & marketing. Live production belongs to form. experience. New software development belongs to form. digital. AI architecture belongs to Solutions & Intelligence.

The rule underneath it: the specialist builds or architects the system; support becomes the steward that helps operate and maintain it. Website maintenance after launch can sit here, escalating into form. digital when engineering is required. AI workflow monitoring after implementation can sit here, escalating into Solutions & Intelligence when the architecture needs to change.

worth saying plainly

we take on other people’s systems.

You don’t have to have built it with us. Inheriting an undocumented environment is normal work here — the first phase is usually an inventory, because you cannot support what nobody has written down.

If what you actually need is a rebuild rather than life support, we will tell you that instead of billing you monthly to hold something together.
