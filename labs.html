how something becomes a product

the fourth time.

Every one of these began as custom work. We solved it once, then again slightly differently, then again. Around the fourth time it becomes obvious that the thing being rebuilt is the same thing, and that rebuilding it by hand is a tax on every future client.

So labs takes it out of the engagement and turns it into a product with an opinion. The engagement work is available today either way — the product is what makes it faster, cheaper and more consistent over time.
