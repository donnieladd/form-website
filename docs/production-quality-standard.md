# Form Intel Website | Public Production Quality Standard
**Status:** Implementation guidance approved 2026-10-09; exact brand source values remain owned by their canonical repositories.

## Identity locked, expressions free

A brand identity's approved masters, geometry, fonts, colors, naming, messaging, visual tokens and master exports are immutable until an authorized owner decision changes them. Do not derive new logos from screenshots or use approximated typefaces.

The identity is the vocabulary; the creative output is not a single template. Site compositions, editorial photography, marketing campaigns, motion, app flows and content can be highly varied while remaining unmistakably within the approved brand.

## No automatic AI layout

Placement, scale, symmetry, asymmetry, negative space, reading sequence, crop, editorial rhythm, hierarchy and alignment must be deliberate. Left, centered, off-center, right or complex compositions are all viable when appropriate. Do not default to generic left-aligned hero blocks, repetitive cards, floating badges or other AI-generated cliches.

Optical balance and the intended emotional experience matter alongside accessibility, legibility, and the realities of mobile and desktop.

## Medium-appropriate production

Evaluate photography, typography, responsive UI, website performance, contact forms and interactions against the actual target environment. Design should be felt through motion, atmosphere, content, tactility and human experience, not only seen as decoration.

For approved artwork production, use genuine source assets, appropriate professional design tools, editable masters when relevant, correct rights and reproducible exports. Do not claim Adobe or other tools were used if they were not.

## Truth, user outcomes and security

- Company claims must be sourced; no invented clients, results, certifications, audits or implied shipped features.
- All public controls must be accessible, responsive and understandable.
- Test empty, error and success states, keyboard/focus, contrast, reduced motion where applicable, actual HTTP handling and privacy boundaries.
- Protect secrets and personal/customer information.
- Demonstrate performance and quality through real build, test and browser evidence, not unverified screenshots or confidence statements.

## Release law

Preserve the approved website experience unless the owner asks for a change. Implement with focused branches and reviewable PRs, correct build and automated tests, accurate documentation and rollback considerations. On this production-linked repository, merging main requires the applicable owner authorization and successful release checks.

**No claim of world-leading execution is evidence by itself.** The result must demonstrate distinctive design, fully working functions, measurable user value and correct operation.
