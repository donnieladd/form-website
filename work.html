a better test than a case study.

Describe a problem you're actually having right now, and we'll tell you how we'd approach it — the mechanism we'd look for first, what we'd build, and roughly what it would take. No charge, no obligation.

That will tell you more about whether we're right for the job than any polished write-up would.
