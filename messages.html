the gap

it isn't a talent problem.

Most churches don't have a creative problem. They have a capacity and continuity problem. The volunteer who makes the graphics is good — they're also working a job, and they see the series title on Thursday.

So the work gets made fast, in isolation, without a system connecting this week to the arc of the series or the series to the year. Nothing is wrong with any single piece. What's missing is the structure underneath them.

That structure is the entire product.
