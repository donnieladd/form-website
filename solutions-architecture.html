six layers

what we design across.

A solution that only touches one of these moves the problem somewhere else. Most failed implementations are a technology answer to a process question.

01

people

Who owns what. Which decisions belong to which role, what capability actually exists today, and what the change will ask of them.

02

process

How work moves. The sequence, the handoffs, the approvals, and the places where things quietly wait for someone who doesn’t know they’re holding it.

03

technology

The platforms, the integrations between them, and the honest question of which existing tools stay, which go, and which were never the problem.

04

data

Where it originates, who is allowed to touch it, where it is duplicated into contradiction, and whether anyone can trust the number in the report.

05

intelligence

What the organization can see about itself, what it can predict, and where AI genuinely augments a decision rather than decorating a dashboard.

06

experience

What all of the above feels like from the outside — to a customer, a guest, a member, or the staff member who has to live inside it every day.
