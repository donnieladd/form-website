what we usually hear

“We built it. Six months later nobody owns it, half of it has drifted, and the person who understood it left.”

the argument

a system built in march is drifting by september.

Not because it was built badly. Because the business changed and the system didn’t. Every implementation quietly assumes someone will keep tending it, and that assumption is usually wrong.
