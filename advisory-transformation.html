how it works

diagnosis before prescription.

[ 01 ]

Understand the operation

We spend time inside how the work actually moves — not how the org chart says it does. Who decides. Where things wait. What people route around. Most of this has never been written down.

[ 02 ]

Name the real constraint

Most organizations are solving a symptom. We identify the actual limiting factor and say so plainly, including when it isn’t the thing you brought us in for.

[ 03 ]

Design the change, and the adoption

Operating model, roles, decision rights, sequence, owners. Plus the adoption plan — because a change nobody adopts didn’t happen, it just got documented.
