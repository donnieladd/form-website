how we work

complex problems don’t live in one department.

Neither should their solutions. Solutions Architecture is the method behind everything below — not another service competing with them. A problem that looks technological is often unclear ownership, a broken handoff, fragmented data or an adoption failure wearing a software costume. So we start with the system: people, process, technology, data, intelligence, experience and operations. Only then does the right combination of capabilities get chosen.

what we don’t do

scope, refused on purpose.

No audit, tax or legal work. No regulatory or forensic engagements. No enterprise cloud operations — DevOps, NOC or FinOps at datacentre scale is a different firm’s job. If your problem is one of those, we’ll say so in the first conversation and point you somewhere real.

And almost no published rate card. Apart from the starting rates named on form. creative & marketing, scope and pricing are set per engagement in a written proposal, because nothing here is honest to quote before the problem is understood.
