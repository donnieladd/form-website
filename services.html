what we don’t do

scope, refused on purpose.

No audit, tax or legal work. No regulatory or forensic engagements. No enterprise cloud operations — DevOps, NOC or FinOps at datacentre scale is a different firm’s job. If your problem is one of those, we’ll say so in the first conversation and point you somewhere real.

And no published rate card: scope and pricing are set per engagement in a written proposal, because nothing here is honest to quote before the problem is understood.
